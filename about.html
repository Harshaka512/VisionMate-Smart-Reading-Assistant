Meet the VisionMate Team

We are final-year undergraduate researchers from the Faculty of Computing at SLIIT, specializing in Information Technology, Edge AI, Computer Vision, and Natural Language Processing.

🎓 SLIIT Faculty of Computing · CDAP Cohort 2026

Research Group R26-IT-41 Verified Roster

Collaborative research cohort developing VisionMate — bringing together Edge AI, Embedded Computer Vision, Multimodal NLP, and Inclusive Assistive Systems. Guided by academic mentors Dr. Lakmini and Ms. Sanjeevi.
