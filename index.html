Research Introduction

The Challenge & Our Breakthrough

Printed media remains one of the largest physical accessibility barriers for individuals with visual impairments.

The Critical Problem

Over 2.2 billion individuals globally endure visual impairments. Despite digital advances, physical printed documents—including textbooks, examination papers, daily newspapers, official correspondence, and medicine prescriptions—remain inaccessible without external human aid.

  • Alignment Challenge: Visually impaired users cannot easily see if the page is tilted, cropped, or out of frame.
  • Information Overload: Long multi-column documents like newspapers are exhausting to read entirely with direct TTS.
  • Monotonic Audio: Standard screen readers ignore reader age, preference, vocabulary level, and tone.

Our Intelligent Solution

VisionMate introduces an end-to-end wearable computing framework powered by edge neural networks and multi-sensory feedback to deliver total reading freedom:

  • Real-Time Camera Guidance: YOLO11n & OpenCV detect document edges, vocally directing camera positioning and auto-capturing frames.
  • Layout & Diagram Explanation: MobileNetV2 classifies documents and multimodal vision describes inside diagrams aloud.
  • Category & Summary Filtering: Ollama LLaMA extracts key article digests from complex multi-topic newspapers.
  • RFID Adaptive Personalization: Personalizes reading level, voice tone, and automatically prompts for page turns.
Research Sub-Domains

Core Individual Research Modules

VisionMate is engineered through four deeply integrated research components developed by our final-year engineering cohort.

Module 01 IT22295392

Automatic Camera Position Guidance & Auto-Capture

Lead Researcher: Priyashantha D G M D
Integrates Raspberry Pi 5 with YOLO11n (0.994 mAP50 on 309 images) and OpenCV boundary refinement to continuously monitor page orientation, delivering real-time voice guidance ("Move closer/left") through dual I2S speakers and executing fast auto-capture when the page is fully visible.

YOLO11n (0.994 mAP50) OpenCV Boundary Distance Calibration Fast Auto-Capture Dual I2S Audio
Module 02 IT22114112

Document Identification and Image Describing

Lead Researcher: D C M A Chithrasena
Classifies 5 document types (Magazine, Newspaper, Novel, Printed Letter, Report) via MobileNetV2 with 87.27% test accuracy (90.91% validation), extracts titles/mastheads via EasyOCR/PaddleOCR, and articulates rich visual descriptions using BLIP & Florence-2 via FastAPI.

MobileNetV2 (87.3%) EasyOCR / PaddleOCR Salesforce BLIP Florence-2 FastAPI
Module 03 IT22198136

Content Summarization & Category Detection

Lead Researcher: Herath H M M G E H B
Reconstructs complex newspaper layouts via Tesseract OCR, OpenCV, and DocLayout-YOLO with graph article reconstruction. Classifies 7 categories via TF-IDF Logistic Regression (85.71% accuracy), provides offline Vosk speech choices, and produces grounded summaries with Llama 3.2:3B via Ollama.

DocLayout-YOLO Graph Article Reconstruction TF-IDF + Logistic Reg (85.7%) Vosk ASR Llama 3.2:3B (Ollama)
Module 04 IT22299970

Personalized Reading and Page Completion Detection

Lead Researcher: Praveesha R G R
Implements persistent RFID-linked SQLite user profiles with a 3-attempt recovery workflow. Fine-tunes T5-small for 3-tier text simplification (BLEU 62.85, 83% accuracy), classifies voice tone using 135-feature PCA-SVM (82.72% accuracy), and signals deterministic spoken page completion.

RFID RC522 + SQLite T5-small Simplification PCA-SVM Voice (82.7%) Spoken Retry Logic Deterministic Page Turn

Physical Prototype

The VisionMate Wearable Device

Custom engineered wearable headgear integrating edge computing, optical guidance sensors, directional acoustics, and RFID personalization into a balanced ergonomic form factor.

Hardware Prototype R26-IT-41
Custom 3D Headset Chassis
Self-Contained Edge Processing Unit
Live Device
01

UGREEN 4K Camera & YOLO11n Guidance

Front-mounted UGREEN 4K USB camera streaming frames to YOLO11n for page detection (0.994 mAP50) with OpenCV boundary refinement, area-ratio distance calibration, and fast auto-capture.

02

Top-Mounted Raspberry Pi 5 Compute Core

Compact chassis housing the Raspberry Pi 5 quad-core ARM processor, powered portably via a power bank through a dedicated power switch and USB Type-C power circuitry.

03

Dual MAX98357 I2S Amplifiers & Stereo Speakers

Two MAX98357 I2S Class-D DAC amplifier breakout modules driving stereo speakers for directional voice guidance ("Move closer/left") and high-fidelity personalized reading playback.

04

Integrated MFRC522 RFID Personalization

Instant contactless card tap links user ID to a local SQLite profile, automatically configuring reading complexity, preferred voice, pace, and tone with spoken retry recovery.
